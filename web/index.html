How Replore works

  
Three steps, right in your browser — no account, no key.

  

  
  
Describe your project, tap Find, and get the repos that fit — relevant, popular, free to use.

  
Build a plan: tick the repos you like on the results, then tap Generate plan.
    Replore hands the picks to your own Claude (Copy, or Open in Claude), which writes a full build plan
    (a PRD) you can code straight from — free and private.

  
For AI-written searches and a whole PRD read feature
    by feature, use the plugin (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any AI agent).

Privacy

  
Replore has no account, no database and no visitor analytics, and stores nothing you type. Its only server
    code is a small relevance check that passes your search to an AI and keeps none of it; if that check fails, it records
    only the error type and time so we can fix it.

  
    
    
  • Your search words go from your browser straight to GitHub (with your IP address) to run the search.
    • 
    
  • To show only relevant repos, your search words and the list of repos found go through our relevance check
      to an AI provider (Cloudflare Workers AI, or Groq when that is unavailable or over its daily limit), which judges which repos fit.
      We don't store or log them; each provider handles them under its own terms
      (Cloudflare,
      Groq). If the check is unavailable you simply
      get the unfiltered keyword results.
    • 
    
  • Bot check: Cloudflare Turnstile is on: your browser loads it from Cloudflare when you search, and
      it checks that you're a person before the AI check runs. It usually needs no click. Cloudflare handles it under the
      same privacy policy.
    • 
    
  • When the page loads, only Cloudflare (our host) sees your IP address; loading the page doesn't reveal what
      you search (the relevance check above does send your search words to Cloudflare). Fonts and
      scripts come from this site, not from Google or other CDNs. Searches are never put in the page address.
    • 
    
  • On your device only: your last search, results and filters, so a refresh brings them back. Tap ✕ in the
      search bar or the Replore logo to delete them.
    • 
    
  • Uploaded plans are read in your browser and never uploaded; only short feature phrases are searched on GitHub.
    • 
    
  • Open in Claude or Open in ChatGPT sends the build-plan prompt to your own claude.ai or chatgpt.com
      account, only when you click it. Open in Gemini copies it and opens gemini.google.com; you paste it.
    • 
    
  • Feedback you send (title, type, message, and your email only if you add it) goes through
      Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com), an email-forwarding service, to hello@cardzo.co.in. Nothing is
      sent until you press Send.
    • 
    
  • No cookies, ads or tracking pixels from Replore.
    • 
  

  
The plugin and MCP server run on your computer; your AI provider handles your
    conversation under its own terms. Full details, including the plugin's local safety log:
    PRIVACY.md.

About the results

  
Results are ranked by fit — how well a repo matches your words, how popular it is (stars),
    and how freely you can use its license — so the top pick is relevant and popular and free to use,
    not just the most-starred. Each card says why it's relevant. This page is keyword search: it matches
    your words, then a small AI check hides repos that clearly don't fit. The
    plugin
    goes further: it has your AI write the searches and judge every result, and reads a whole plan
    feature by feature. Results are GitHub search matches, not a scan of every repository. Stars measure
    popularity, not quality, and can be bought. "Completely free?" and "OK in a paid product?" are read from the
    license only — a plain summary, not legal advice; some projects also sell a paid hosted version or add-ons.
